FAQs
1. Who can participate in Strategia?
 Both undergraduate and postgraduate students currently enrolled in a recognized college or university can participate.
2. How many members are required in a team?
 Each event requires a team of 3 members. Individual participation is not allowed.
3. Can a team register for more than one event?
 No. One team can register for only one event. This ensures fair competition and commitment to the chosen track.
4. What is the registration fee?
 The registration fee is ₹300 per team. Payment must be completed on the official college portal after registering on the Strategia website.
5. Is the registration fee refundable?
 No. The registration fee is non-refundable under any circumstances.
6. Who needs to complete the registration?
 Only one member from each team needs to register on the Strategia website on behalf of the entire team. Payment can also be completed by the same member.
7. What is the dress code for participants?
 Formal attire is mandatory for all participants throughout the event.
8. Are laptops and calculators allowed?
 Yes. For events like Case Vault, Portfolio Playbook, and Boardroom Blitz, teams must carry their own laptops and calculators. Internet access will be provided during the events.
9. How will participants be notified about round timings and updates?
 All important announcements, round schedules, and updates will be shared via the official Strategia WhatsApp groups and email communication once registration is confirmed.
10. What is the Final Showdown?
 The Final Showdown brings together the top 2 teams from each event. The challenge format is revealed on the spot and is designed to test strategic, financial, and leadership skills. The winning team will be crowned Strategia’26 Champion.
11. Will certificates be provided?
 Yes. Certificates of participation will be given to all teams, and special certificates will be awarded to winners and finalists.
12. What happens if there is a dispute during the competition?
 All grievances must be submitted to the core team. The decisions of the judges and the organizing committee are final and binding.
13. Are accommodation and food provided?
 Accommodation is not provided by default. However, assistance will be offered for participants traveling from outside Chennai. Food arrangements will be available on campus during the event days.
14. Do participants need to bring their own stationery?
 Writing materials for quiz rounds will be provided. For other events, participants should bring any required materials like laptops, chargers, and calculators.
15. How are the winners decided in each event?
 Winners are decided based on event-specific evaluation criteria such as accuracy, reasoning, presentation quality, creativity, and financial performance, as judged by the expert panel.
