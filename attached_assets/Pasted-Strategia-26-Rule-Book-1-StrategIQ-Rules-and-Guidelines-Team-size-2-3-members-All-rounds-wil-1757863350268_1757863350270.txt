Strategia’26 Rule Book
1. StrategIQ
Rules and Guidelines:
Team size: 2-3 members.
All rounds will be conducted offline.
Teams must adhere to the time limits set for each round.
The decisions of the judges and organizing committee are final and binding.
Day 1 - Round 1: Written Quiz
A one-hour written quiz covering concepts from finance, business, and general knowledge.
The qualified teams will proceed to Round 2.
Day 1 - Round 2: Strategic Justification
Teams will be presented with a strategic scenario containing multiple options.
Teams must select a pathway and present their justification to the panel.
Evaluation will be based on choice of option, quality of reasoning, and clarity of communication.
The qualified teams will proceed to Round 3.
Day 2 - Round 3: Buzzer Round Finale
A live buzzer quiz featuring multiple formats such as rapid fire, connections, and more.
Scores will be displayed on a leaderboard, and the team with the highest score will be declared the winners.
Top 2 teams qualify for the Final Showdown.
2. Market Masters
Rules and Guidelines:
Team size: 2-3 members.
Teams must bring laptops, calculators, and essentials.
Malpractice (external funds, trade deletion, etc.) will lead to disqualification.
Teams must adhere to the presentation time limits.
The decisions of the judges and organizing committee are final and binding.
Day 1 - Round 1: Virtual Trading
Teams receive virtual capital via the simulation app.
Trading begins from 10:00 a.m. until the market closes.
Rankings are based on net portfolio returns at the end of Day 1.
The qualified teams will proceed to Round 2.
The qualified teams are assigned a financial persona (e.g., young professional, mid-career, retiree) by 5:00 p.m.
Day 2 - Round 2: Portfolio Management
Teams will have overnight preparation to design a portfolio, utilizing any asset class to create the optimal mix.
Presentation Time: 7 minutes + 3 minutes Q&A.
Top 2 teams qualify for the Final Showdown.

3. VentureX
Rules and Guidelines:
Team size: 2-3 members.
Participants are only required to register on the Strategia website for the preliminary round. Further instructions regarding submission and payment will be provided via email after initial registration.
No registration fee is required for initial pitchdeck submission.
Teams must adhere to the submission & presentation time limits.
The decisions of the judges and organizing committee are final and binding.
Online - Round 1: Pitch Deck Submission
Teams submit an 8-slide deck.
Deadline: 11:59 PM, December 31, 2025.
The qualified teams will proceed to Round 2.
Qualified teams must pay the registration fee to confirm their participation.
Unqualified teams may register for one of the other three events, provided slots are available.
Day 1 - Round 2: Live Pitching
Teams will pitch their business ideas on Day 1.
Time Limit: 6 minutes + 3 minutes Q&A.
The qualified teams will proceed for Demo Day.
Day 2 - Demo Day
Teams present their Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) to investors and industry leaders.
Top 2 teams qualify for the Final Showdown.
4. Case Quest
Rules and Guidelines:
Team size: 2-3 members.
Teams must bring laptops, calculators, and essentials.
Internet facilities will be made available during the event.
All submissions and presentations must be original and solely prepared by the team.
Teams must adhere strictly to submission and presentation timelines.
The decisions of the judges and organizing committee are final and binding.
Day 1 - Round 1: Preliminary Case Submission
The case study will be released on the spot at the start of the event.
Teams must prepare their solution within the campus and submit their deck by 2:00 PM.
The shortlisted teams will receive the final case study by 5:00 PM for overnight preparation.
Teams must prepare their final deck overnight and submit it before the start of the final round on Day 2.
Day 2 - Round 2: Final Presentations
Teams will present their solutions to a closed panel of judges, ensuring fairness and confidentiality.
Time Limit: 7 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A.
Top 2 teams will receive the ticket to the Final Showdown.
Final Showdown
Rules and Guidelines:
The top two teams from each event will qualify for the Final Showdown.
The nature of the challenge will remain undisclosed until the event begins.
Rules and guidelines will be provided on the spot.
Participants will be tested on their ability to think on their feet, collaborate effectively, and perform under pressure.
