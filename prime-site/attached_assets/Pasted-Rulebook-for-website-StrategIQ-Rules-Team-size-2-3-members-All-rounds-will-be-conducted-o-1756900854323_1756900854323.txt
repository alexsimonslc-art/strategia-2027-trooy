Rulebook for website
StrategIQ 
Rules
Team size: 2-3 members.


All rounds will be conducted offline.


Writing materials will be provided.


Time limits are strict and must be followed.


Judges’ decisions are final and binding.


Event Flow
Day 1


Round 1 – Written Quiz (1 hour): Questions from finance, business, and general awareness. Top 20 teams qualify.


Round 2 – Strategic Justification: Teams face a scenario with multiple options, choose one path, and justify it to the panel. Evaluation is based on reasoning, clarity, and communication. Top 6 teams qualify for the finals.


Day 2


Finale – Live Buzzer Quiz: Includes rapid fire, connections, and other formats. Leaderboard will display scores. The top 2 teams qualify for the Final Showdown.




Case Quest 
Rules
Team size: 2-3 members.


Teams must bring laptops, calculators, and required materials.


Internet will be available during the event.


Submissions must be original and made strictly within deadlines; late entries will not be accepted.


Judges’ decisions are final.


Event Flow
Day 1


Preliminary Case: Case released on the spot at event start. Teams prepare and submit their deck by 2:00 PM. Late submissions won’t be accepted.


Final Case Release: Shortlisted teams receive a new case at 5:00 PM for overnight preparation. Decks must be ready before the start of Day 2 presentations.


Day 2


Final Presentations: Each team gets 7 minutes to present, followed by 5 minutes of Q&A. Judges evaluate originality, structure, practicality, and delivery.


Top 2 teams move to the Final Showdown.


Market Masters 
Rules
Team size: 2-3 members.


Teams must bring laptops, calculators, and essentials.


Internet access will be provided.


All submissions and trades must respect the given timelines.


Judges’ decisions are final.



Event Flow
Day 1


Live Virtual Trading: From 10:30 AM until market close, teams trade using the platform provided. Evaluation is based only on portfolio performance and returns.


Client Profile Release: At 5:00 PM, shortlisted teams receive a client profile. All teams receive the same profile for fairness and must prepare tailored strategies overnight.


Day 2


Portfolio Presentations: Teams present strategies from 9:00 AM. Each team gets 7 minutes to present, plus 5 minutes Q&A.


Judges evaluate portfolio design, risk-return balance, and client alignment.


Top 2 teams qualify for the Final Showdown.


VentureX 
Rules
Team size: 2-3 members.


Submissions must be original and meet deadlines.


The final date for pitch deck submission is 11:59 PM, January 23, 2026.


Shortlisted teams must confirm registration and payment through the official portal.


Judges’ decisions are final.


Event Flow
Prelims


Pitch Deck Submission: Open to all registered teams. Top 25 are shortlisted.


Day 1


Live Pitches: Shortlisted teams present before judges (7 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A). Top 6 qualify for Demo Day.


Day 2


Demo Day: The top 6 teams pitch to real VCs and a live audience (7 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A).


Judges and VCs evaluate innovation, scalability, and financial viability.


Top 2 teams qualify for the Final Showdown.
