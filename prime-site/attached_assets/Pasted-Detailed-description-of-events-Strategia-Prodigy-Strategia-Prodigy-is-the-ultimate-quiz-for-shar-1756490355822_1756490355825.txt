Detailed description of events

Strategia Prodigy
Strategia Prodigy is the ultimate quiz for sharp minds in business, finance, and general awareness. The event unfolds across a written quiz, scenario-based justifications, and a high-energy buzzer finale. All rounds are conducted offline, materials are provided, and the judges’ decisions remain final. Only the smartest and fastest teams advance to the Final Showdown. Refer rulebook for details.
Key Skills Required
Business and financial knowledge


Quick thinking and recall


Clear articulation


Team coordination
Strategia Case Vault
Strategia Case Vault puts participants in the shoes of consultants, tasked with solving complex business problems and defending their recommendations. Teams prepare case decks under tight deadlines, with a preliminary case on Day 1 and a final case presented before judges on Day 2. Judges’ decisions are binding, and only the most strategic teams move forward to the Final Showdown. Refer rulebook for details.
Key Skills Required
Problem-solving and analysis


Logical and strategic reasoning


Presentation and persuasion


Time management
Portfolio Playbook
Portfolio Playbook is a live market and wealth management challenge where teams test their trading instincts and portfolio strategies. Teams begin with a full-day virtual trading simulation, evaluated purely on returns, before moving on to client-focused portfolio presentations on Day 2. Judges’ verdicts are final, and only the top-performing teams secure a place in the Final Showdown. Refer rulebook for details.
Key Skills Required
Market and financial acumen


Risk management


Strategic thinking


Communication skills
Boardroom Blitz
Boardroom Blitz is Strategia’s shark tank style event, where entrepreneurial vision meets investor scrutiny. Teams submit pitch decks in advance, with the best moving on to live rounds where they present ideas before judges, culminating in Demo Day with real venture capitalists and a live audience. The stakes are high, the atmosphere intense, and only the boldest pitches advance to the Final Showdown. Refer rulebook for details.
Key Skills Required
Entrepreneurship and innovation


Strategic planning


Persuasive pitching


Investor-focused communication
Final Showdown
The Final Showdown is the grand stage where the top teams from every event compete head-to-head in a challenge revealed on the spot. Designed to test the complete skill set of a modern leader, the finale demands adaptability, problem-solving, financial acumen, and sharp decision-making under pressure. The best of the best will emerge as the Strategia Champion.
Key Skills Required
Adaptability and leadership


Problem-solving under pressure


Financial and strategic acumen


Team synergy
