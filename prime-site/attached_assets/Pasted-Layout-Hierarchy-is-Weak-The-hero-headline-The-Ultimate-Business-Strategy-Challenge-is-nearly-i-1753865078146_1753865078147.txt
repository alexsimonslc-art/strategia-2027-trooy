Layout Hierarchy is Weak

The hero headline ("The Ultimate Business Strategy Challenge") is nearly invisible — it needs better contrast and positioning.

Prize pool box overlaps the event title/logo awkwardly — looks cluttered and unbalanced.

Poor Visual Balance

Too many bold/primary elements fighting for attention (Prize Pool, Countdown, Buttons).

The countdown timer has low contrast against the blue background.

Bad Spacing and Margins

Spacing between the different sections (title, prize pool, countdown) is too tight — looks congested.

No clear separation between functional blocks (event info, CTA, timer).

Prize Pool Card Design is Outdated

Harsh drop shadow + red border combo looks outdated.

Could be cleaner with a subtle blur or gradient glassmorphism and hover animation.

Call-to-Actions (CTAs) Clash

Two CTAs (“Register Now” and “View Events & Prizes”) stacked horizontally confuse the user.

Primary CTA should be just one — bold, centered, with the second CTA styled as secondary (ghost button or link style).

No Speaker Tease / Affiliation

You’ve mentioned hinting hot speakers & adding ACCA – currently missing.
