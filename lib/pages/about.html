About the College

Loyola College, Chennai, established in 1925 by the Society of Jesus, stands today as one of the most prestigious institutions of higher education in India. Loyola has become synonymous with academic rigor, intellectual freedom, and transformative education. Home to over 10,000 students across diverse undergraduate and postgraduate programs, it continues to shape leaders to excel globally. Ranked 8th in the 2024 NIRF listings and accredited with the highest NAAC grade of ‘A++’, Loyola is recognized as a “College of Excellence.”

Beyond rankings, its vibrant campus culture, pioneering research, and holistic development initiatives create an unparalleled ecosystem where students thrive not only as scholars but as innovators, changemakers, and global citizens.

About the Department

The Department of B.Com.(Honours) was introduced at Loyola College in 2018. This program falls under the purview of the Loyola - International Academic Collaboration (LIAC), which is dedicated to fostering a global mindset among its students. B.Com (Honours) is an embedded program with ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants) and semester-end internships. Known for its culture of excellence, industry exposure, and innovation, the department has built a reputation for producing future-ready leaders.

Its flagship cultural fest, Imperium, showcases the department’s vibrant spirit, while Strategia reflects its drive for intellectual and professional distinction. The B.Com.(Honours) department is distinguished by its unique blend of energy, vision, and achievement.

About STRATEGIA

Strategia is B.Com. Honours’s flagship competition and one of the most formidable platforms for aspiring business leaders in the country. Designed to mirror the intensity of the corporate world, it brings together the sharpest minds to battle across consulting challenges, trading simulations, entrepreneurial pitches, and high-stakes quizzes, culminating in the Final Showdown.

Each edition sets new benchmarks in scale, quality, and impact, drawing participants from premier institutions nationwide. Backed by the legacy of Loyola’s B.Com.(Honours) department, Strategia is where intellect meets pressure, strategy meets execution, and leaders are made.
