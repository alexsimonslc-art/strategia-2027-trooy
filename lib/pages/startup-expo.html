StartupExpo @ Strategia 2026

Where Startups, Investors & Students Converge

February 2 & 3, 2026
Loyola College, Chennai

What is StartupExpo?

StartupExpo is where early-stage startups, scaling ventures, investors, and a high-intent student audience come together in one focused business environment.

For Founders

Over two days, founders get the chance to:

  • Pitch and interact with VCs and ecosystem leaders
  • Test products with real users
  • Build partnerships with fellow founders and brands
  • Create serious campus-level brand presence

For Students

Direct exposure to real startups, real products, and real conversations with people building companies on the ground. Network with founders, learn about different business models, and discover potential career opportunities.

Why Exhibit at StartupExpo?

Direct Market Access
