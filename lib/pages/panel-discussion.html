Panel Discussion

Thinking Like a Business Leader in the age of AI

Lessons Everyone Should Know

February 03, 2026
1:00 PM - 5:30 PM IST
Bertram Hall

Ever wondered what separates business leaders from the rest?

It's not just what they know — IT'S HOW THEY THINK.

Before the panel begins, participants will take part in a high-energy Guesstimate Challenge, where you’ll solve real-world estimation problems under time pressure, just like consultants and startup teams do. It’s fast, competitive, and designed to push your business instincts.

After that, experienced professionals and leaders will break down the real lessons of business leadership: making decisions under pressure, owning your wins and losses, spotting opportunities others miss, and navigating the rise of AI in business. No fluff, just practical insights you can apply right now.

What We're Covering

Strategic Thinking

Learn to think beyond tasks and see the bigger picture

AI in Business

Why AI literacy matters for your future career

Ownership & Accountability

Why taking responsibility separates leaders from followers

Problem Solving

Approach challenges like a seasoned business leader

Building Networks

Create meaningful connections that accelerate your career

Real-World Insights

Practical lessons from experienced business professionals

Who Should Come?

Anyone serious about building a real career in business.

Whether you want to lead teams, start something, or make an impact in corporate — this session will give you the frameworks and mindsets that separate successful leaders from the rest.
